# NEXORA

> **AI-Powered Enterprise ERP & Quality Engineering Platform**

NEXORA is an open, enterprise-grade ERP platform designed to demonstrate modern Software Development, Quality Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and Enterprise Architecture in a real-world environment.

The project serves as the practical implementation platform behind my **QA in the AI Era** series, where modern AI technologies and Quality Engineering practices are applied to solve realistic enterprise challenges.

---

# 🌍 Vision

To build an open, enterprise-grade Quality Engineering platform where developers, QA engineers, students, and AI enthusiasts can **learn, practice, experiment, and gain real-world experience** through enterprise-scale scenarios—not just tutorials or sample applications.

---

# 🎯 Mission

NEXORA bridges the gap between learning and real-world enterprise software by providing a practical environment to:

- Build complete ERP modules from the ground up.
- Demonstrate enterprise software architecture and design.
- Explore AI-powered Quality Engineering.
- Practice end-to-end Software Development and Testing.
- Experiment with modern AI technologies in enterprise applications.
- Share practical knowledge and best practices with the community.
