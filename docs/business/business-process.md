# Business Process

## Procurement Management Business Process

The Procurement Management module manages the complete lifecycle of purchasing goods from suppliers.

The objective is to ensure that purchases are controlled, approved, traceable, and accurately recorded.

---

# Objectives

- Standardise procurement activities
- Improve supplier management
- Reduce manual processing
- Ensure approval compliance
- Provide complete audit history

---

# High-Level Process

Supplier

↓

Purchase Order Creation

↓

Purchase Order Validation

↓

Purchase Order Submission

↓

Manager Approval

↓

Approved Purchase Order

↓

Goods Receipt (Future Phase)

↓

Inventory Update (Future Phase)

↓

Financial Processing (Future Phase)

# Business Actors

- Procurement Officer
- Procurement Manager
- Supplier
- System Administrator

---

# Inputs

- Supplier information
- Purchase order details
- Product information
- Quantities
- Prices

---

# Outputs

- Purchase Order
- Approval Decision
- Purchase Order Status
- Audit History

---

# Success Criteria

A successful procurement process should:

- Create purchase orders successfully
- Validate business rules
- Require manager approval
- Maintain complete audit history
- Prevent unauthorised approvals
